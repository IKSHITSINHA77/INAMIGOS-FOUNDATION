Who we are

Bridging the gap between resource abundance and community deprivation.

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla, headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. What began as a local response to hunger, illiteracy and neglect has grown into a nationwide movement — one that bypasses middle-layers so aid reaches the actual beneficiaries directly.

We build localized, community-driven programmes for education, environment, gender equality, animal welfare and skill acquisition — pairing young, enthusiastic volunteers with experienced social workers to design, test and lead development models at the grassroots level.

Where the work happens

Six roots, one tree of change.

Every project targets a specific gap in society. Together, they form a complete ecosystem of care that grows outward from local communities to the national scale.

Project SEVA
Food & Clothing Distribution

Regular drives delivering hygienic, freshly-cooked meals and clothing to homeless families and slum communities — because no one should sleep hungry.

Project Bachpanshala
Education for Every Child

Free learning spaces for children shut out of quality schooling by poverty — because every young mind deserves knowledge and hope.

Project Udaan
Women's Empowerment

Skill training and financial tools that help women become independent, confident contributors to their households and communities.

Project Prakriti
Environment & Plantation

Tree plantation drives held every monsoon and on World Environment Day, working toward cleaner air and a more sustainable future.

Project Jeev
Animal Welfare

Feeding drives and rescue support for strays and wildlife — spreading the belief that compassion extends to every living creature.

Project Vikas
Skill Development

Digital and professional skill-building for youth and interns, turning volunteering into real leadership and career experience.

Why it matters

We treat root causes, not just symptoms.

In a world that often feels divided, InAmigos works to restore unity and dignity — tackling the lack of education, the loss of green cover, the absence of women's empowerment, and the neglect of animal welfare all at once, at the community level where change actually sticks.

Over 30,000 interns and youth volunteers have already helped design, test and lead these community development models — proof that grassroots effort, multiplied, becomes a movement.
